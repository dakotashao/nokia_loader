Fling Loader

A progress indicator where progress is a physical rule: the bird can only fly as far as the task has come.

63% sent → bird flies 63%
100% → the wall is gone

Concept

The problemAI tasks make people wait, and a bar that creeps along gives them nothing to do and little reason to trust it.

The moveTurn the percentage into an invisible wall in a tiny slingshot game. Each attempt shows, by feel, how far the task has come.

The frameA send-message flow on a 2001-era monochrome phone: familiar, bounded, and quiet enough to sit inside a real interface.

Iterations

v1

The brief, taken literally

A cartoon slingshot scene above a standard progress bar. The core rule was already there: the task's percentage sets an invisible wall, so the bird can only fly as far as the task has progressed.

Dakota's feedback
你现在设计的有点太复杂了…我希望你做成诺基亚像素风Too much animation for a small progress bar. Make it Nokia pixel style.
v2

First pixel pass

A 1-bit, low-resolution canvas with a blocky segmented bar and a boxed soft key.

Dakota's feedback
Less like a pixel arcade game, more like a premium, modern reinterpretation of a 2001 Nokia. Integrate progress into the bird's flight path. Use soft-key labels.
v3

Progress moves onto the flight path

The segmented bar was removed. One pixel rail runs from slingshot to target, and a notch on it marks the wall. Olive LCD, grey bezel, BACK / FIRE soft keys.

Dakota's feedback
Use the attached image as the visual source of truth.
Input

Dakota's storyboard

Dakota defined the full story in 12 frames: confirm, sending, launch attempts at 12 / 24 / 42 / 63 / 87 %, the wall disappearing at 100 %, the bird reaching the mailbox, a success message and the return to messages. It fixed the layout, the mailbox and the tone.

v4 – v7

Matching the storyboard

Rebuilt as one continuous flow: SEND THIS PICTURE? → SENDING... → game → MESSAGE SENT → OK. The work then became pixel-level comparison against the reference: screen grid density, 2-pixel letter spacing, the slingshot, bird and mailbox redrawn, and the wall's impact marks.

Dakota's feedback
不行你这个视觉请完全还原我的Match my visuals exactly.
你要不然自己找找差距？另外画质不需要那么清晰Find the gaps yourself. It doesn't need to be that crisp.
v8 – v10

Into a real handset

The font changed to VT323, and hovering a soft key inverts it to a black block, as on the original UI. The phone went from a coded clear-plastic shell, to Dakota's handset photo, to a silver handset. The LCD colour, soft falloff and grain were sampled from that photo.

Dakota's feedback
放进这里面不要改我的屏幕Put it in this phone — don't change my screen.
请完全参考这个手机上的屏幕的颜色以及高清程度Match this phone's screen colour and resolution.
Input

Dakota's bird sprite sheet

A frame-by-frame sheet (idle, stretch, launch, hit, fall, reset) that replaced the generic bird.

v11 – v20

Details that make it feel native

A status bar with signal, local time and battery. The bird is drawn from the sprite sheet. The copy became SEND THIS MESSAGE?. The progress notch was replaced by dots that fill in as the send advances. Status copy was shortened to PACKING... / EN ROUTE... / CLOSER... / MADE IT!, and an outgoing-message envelope was added. The screen was fitted into the bezel so no edge shows.

Dakota's feedback
on the way这个文字看起来好长'On the way' is too long.
去掉那个表示到哪里的竖线，改成加载虚线点的小动画Drop the marker; let the dots themselves load.
v21 – v33

Camera and pacing

A full-bleed stage. The whole phone shows at rest, the camera leans in over 3 s, and pulls back after OK. Zoom-on-hover was tried and rejected: the camera now waits until the user clicks SEND. Loading time was halved, from about 20 s to about 10 s, so nobody gives up waiting.

Dakota's feedback
放大的有点快了The zoom is a bit fast.
我还是改成用户点击send以后屏幕再放大吧Only zoom in after the user clicks SEND.
进度条从0%-100%的时间砍半Halve the 0–100 % time.
Variant

Indeterminate version

When progress can't be measured, the wall has nothing to stand on. Instead, three dots sweep the rail, the percentage becomes an elapsed timer, and the status reassures the longer it takes (STILL FLYING, HANG TIGHT). The bird flies freely, but the mailbox stays shut (NOT YET...) until the task finishes and the flag pops up. No fake percentage anywhere.

What I tried and rolled back

Blur and vignette for an 'aged' LCDRemoved: aging should come from colour and grain, not softness.
A fixed 1980 × 1020 stage with zoom on hoverReverted in the next turn.
Bottom row aligned to the slingshot and mailboxReverted: text stays on the system margin.
A bigger bird, larger status icons and taller propsReverted, then the bird alone grew by one pixel.
A 4:3 framed stageReplaced by a full-bleed background.

Principles that emerged

Progress is a physical rule.

The percentage isn't only displayed; it limits what the bird can do. Waiting becomes something you can feel and test.

Honest when you can't know.

The indeterminate version drops the percentage instead of faking one. It shows elapsed time and reassures the user instead.

The system waits for intent.

The camera stays still until the user commits by clicking SEND, and returns when they press OK.

Restraint over spectacle.

Every round removed something: comic text, confetti, scenery, a bar, a marker.
